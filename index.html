Frequently asked questions

What does a Digital Marketing Analyst do?

I look at how people find and use your website, then make changes to bring in more of the right visitors and turn them into enquiries. That covers SEO, analytics, reporting, CRM and automation.

Which businesses can you help?

I can help small and growing businesses in the UAE that want more enquiries from search, better tracking of where leads come from, or a more organised way to handle them. That includes local shops and service businesses as well as B2B companies.

Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. I can show you what is holding your site back and what to do about it.

What is the first step?
