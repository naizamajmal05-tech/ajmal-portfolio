Website Audits for UAE businesses

A focused review of your website with a short, ordered list of fixes, so you know what to do first and why.

What I do

  • Technical SEO review: indexing, speed, mobile, security and structure
  • On-page review of titles, headings, content and internal links
  • Check of local signals and your Google Business Profile
  • Check that analytics and conversion tracking work
  • A written report with fixes ranked by impact and effort

Who it is for

Business owners who suspect their website is underperforming, are planning a redesign, or want a second opinion on work done by an agency or freelancer.

I carry out website SEO audits in the UAE that show what is working, what is not and what to fix first.

How it works

  1. You send me the website address and a few details about your goals.
  2. I review the site with a mix of tools and manual checks.
  3. You receive a written report in plain language.
  4. We go through it together, and I can help implement the fixes if you wish.

Frequently asked questions

Will I get a technical document I cannot understand?

No. Each finding says what the problem is, why it matters and what to do about it.

Do I have to hire you to fix things afterwards?

No. The report is yours to give to your developer or to use yourself.

What is a website audit?

A review of your website that shows what is working, what is not and what to fix first.

What does the audit cover?

Technical SEO (indexing, speed, mobile, security and structure), on-page content, local signals and your Google Business Profile, and whether analytics and conversion tracking work.

What will I receive?

A written report in plain language, with fixes ranked by impact and effort.

Is an audit useful before a redesign?

Yes. It shows what to keep, what to fix and what to avoid before you spend on a new site.

Can you review work done by another agency or freelancer?

Yes. A second opinion on work done by an agency or freelancer is one of the most common reasons for an audit.

What do you need from me?

Your website address and a few details about your goals.
