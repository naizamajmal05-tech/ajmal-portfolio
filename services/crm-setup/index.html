CRM Setup for UAE businesses

A CRM stops leads falling through the cracks. I set one up around how your team already works, and keep it simple enough that people actually use it.

What I do

  • Choosing a CRM that fits your size and budget
  • Setting up contacts, deal stages and custom fields
  • Connecting website forms, email and WhatsApp enquiries
  • Reminders and templates for follow-ups
  • Automated lead capture and follow-up workflows, built with tools such as n8n
  • Short training and a one-page guide for your team

Who it is for

Small teams and owner-managed businesses in the UAE that currently track leads in chats, spreadsheets or memory, and want a more reliable process.

How it works

  1. I map how enquiries reach you and what happens next.
  2. We agree a simple pipeline with clear stages.
  3. I configure the system and connect your lead sources.
  4. I train your team and check in after the first few weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Which CRM should I choose?

It depends on your team size, budget and tools. I will compare a few suitable options and recommend one, with reasons.

Can leads from my website go straight into the CRM?

Yes. Forms, and in many cases WhatsApp and email enquiries, can be captured automatically.
