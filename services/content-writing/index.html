Content Writing for UAE businesses

Content that answers the questions your customers ask, written plainly and structured so Google can understand it.

What I do

  • Service and location page copy
  • Blog articles that target real customer questions
  • Rewrites of existing pages that are thin or unclear
  • Titles, meta descriptions and internal linking
  • FAQ sections written to be genuinely useful

Who it is for

Small businesses that know what they offer but struggle to put it into words, or that have not had time to keep their website and blog up to date.

I provide SEO content writing in the UAE: helpful pages and articles written for people and search.

How it works

  1. You share what you know about your services and customers.
  2. I research the questions and phrases people search for.
  3. I draft the copy and send it for your comments.
  4. After your approval, the content is formatted for publishing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the content original?

Yes. Everything is written from scratch for your business, based on your input and my research.

What content do you write?

Service and location page copy, blog articles that target real customer questions, rewrites of existing pages, titles and meta descriptions, and FAQ sections.

Do you write for search engines or for people?

Both. Content has to be helpful to people first, and structured so search engines can understand it.

Do you research keywords before writing?

Yes. I research the questions and phrases people search for before I write.

Can you rewrite my existing pages?

Yes. Pages that are thin or unclear can be rewritten, along with their titles, meta descriptions and internal links.

Will I get to review the content?

Yes. I send the draft for your comments, and it is formatted for publishing only after your approval.

What do you need from me?

What you know about your services and customers. I handle the research and writing from there.

Do you publish the content on my website?

I format the approved content ready for publishing. Tell me how you would like it added to your site and we will agree the best way.

Ready to talk?

Send me your website address and what you want to achieve. I will reply with a suggested next step and no obligation.
