Analytics & Reporting for UAE businesses

Numbers are only useful when they lead to a decision. I set up tracking you can trust and report it in a way you can act on.

What I do

  • Analytics setup and checks
  • Tracking for calls, WhatsApp clicks, forms and key buttons
  • A clear dashboard with only the figures that matter
  • Clean-up of tracking that is missing or double-counted

Who it is for

Owners and managers who spend money or time on marketing and want to know what is actually working, without reading a 40-page report.

How it works

  1. I review what is currently tracked and what you need to know.
  2. I fix and extend the tracking setup.
  3. I build a dashboard around your goals.
  4. Each reporting period you receive a short summary and next steps.

Frequently asked questions

Which tools do you use?

Mainly Excel and Power BI. I can work with what you already have.

Will you replace my existing reports?

Only if they are not helping. I would rather simplify than add more charts.
