SEO for UAE businesses

Practical SEO that helps people in the UAE find your business on Google, explained without jargon and without empty promises.

What I do

  • Keyword research based on how your customers really search
  • On-page improvements to titles, headings, copy and internal links
  • Technical checks covering speed, mobile layout, indexing and structured data
  • Local SEO signals for the emirates and neighbourhoods you serve

Who it is for

Owner-run shops, clinics, restaurants, trades, consultancies and B2B companies in the UAE that have a website but are not receiving enough enquiries from search.

How it works

  1. We talk about your business, your best customers and your goals.
  2. I audit the site and build a keyword map that matches real search demand.
  3. We fix the highest-impact pages first, then work through the rest.
  4. New or improved content is published against that plan.
  5. I track results and adjust the plan.

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to work?

Usually several months before you see steady movement, and it depends on competition and the current state of your site. I will not promise a fixed ranking or a fixed date.

Do I need a new website?

Often not. Many sites can be improved in place. If something on the site is holding results back, I will tell you plainly and explain the options.

Can you also handle my Google Business Profile?

Yes. Local search and website SEO work best together, so I treat them as one plan.

Send me your website address and what you want to achieve. I will reply with a suggested next step and no obligation.
