AI Automation for UAE businesses

Practical automations that remove repetitive work, such as lead alerts, follow-ups and reports, so your team can spend time on customers.

What I do

  • Automatic lead capture from forms into your CRM or spreadsheet
  • Instant alerts and follow-up messages for new enquiries
  • AI-assisted drafts for replies, summaries and routine reports, using tools such as Claude and ChatGPT
  • Scheduled reports sent to your inbox
  • Documentation so you know exactly what each automation does

Who it is for

Small teams that lose time to copying data between tools, chasing follow-ups or building the same report every week.

How it works

  1. We list the tasks that take the most time and pick the best candidates.
  2. I design a simple workflow and show you before building, using tools such as n8n.
  3. I build and test it with real examples, with a human check where needed.
  4. I hand over documentation and monitor the first weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Will AI replace my staff?

No. The aim is to remove repetitive admin so people have more time for the work only they can do.

Is my customer data safe?

I only connect tools you already trust, limit the data each one sees and explain what is shared. For sensitive data we will agree the approach first.
